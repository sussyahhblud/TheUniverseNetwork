ℹ️ How to Use

Step 1: Select the console/system you want to emulate from the dropdown menu

Step 2: Click "Select ROM File" and choose your game ROM file from your computer

Step 3: Click "Load and Play Game" to start playing!

Controls: Use your keyboard or connect a gamepad. Default keyboard controls are typically Arrow Keys (D-Pad), Z/X (A/B), Enter (Start), Shift (Select)

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Only use ROM files that you legally own
  • Some systems (PlayStation, Sega CD, Saturn) may require BIOS files
  • Your game saves are stored locally in your browser
  • For best performance, use modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
